Continue
Peptides.

A premium peptide brand built to earn trust from day one.

UI/UXGraphicWebsiteAds
Read the story +

Overview

Continue Peptides brings clinic-level health solutions to busy, health-focused customers. The design needed to feel premium and approachable while earning trust for a new brand.

The challenge

  • Make a new brand feel established without becoming cold or clinical.
  • Create ads for a restricted category while following platform advertising policies.

The process

  1. Researched peptide, supplement, and premium wellness competitors.
  2. Defined a clean, premium visual direction with approachable imagery.
  3. Mapped the landing-to-checkout journey and designed the website in Figma.
  4. Reviewed ad platform policies and adjusted the visual and copy approach.

The solution

Website design, consistent product thumbnails, and static ads and social posts built around the premium brand direction.

Outcome

Delivered the website design and ad creative system for the 2026 launch phase. Website visuals shown here will use Figma screenshots; the live site was redesigned by another designer.

Overview

HugGooling introduces the Indonesian guling, a familiar hug pillow, to Australian customers. The brand needed to explain an unfamiliar product and make it feel desirable.

The challenge

  • Explain what a gooling is, how it is used, and why it is worth having.
  • Build trust and give a new product a warm, lovable personality.

The process

  1. Researched Australian sleep and home shopping behavior.
  2. Set a cute, soft, warm visual direction.
  3. Created the brand mascot to make the product familiar and memorable.
  4. Enhanced real product photography with Higgsfield while keeping it true to the product.
  5. Designed an education-first website in Figma.
  6. Designed mascot-led packaging, ads, and social content.

The solution

A mascot-led brand experience spanning the live website, product photography, packaging, ads, and social media.

Outcome

Led creative direction and built the brand experience for the Australian launch. Visit huggooling.com ↗

Mascot artwork and early sketches are still needed for the final visuals.

STORY 032023–25

PAW
Studio.

Turning beloved pets into art that lasts forever.

IllustrationUI/UXSocial300+ portraits
Read the story +

Overview

PAW Studio turns customers' pets into printed keepsakes. Across two years I illustrated pets, designed the website and content, managed social media, and ran the store.

The challenge

  • Show customers that each pet's markings and personality could be captured accurately.
  • Build trust for custom orders paid before the artwork is delivered.
  • Communicate the emotional value clearly across the website and social content.

The process

  1. Hand-illustrated portraits in Photoshop, matching fur patterns, eye color, and expression.
  2. Used original-photo and finished-art comparisons as visible proof of accuracy.
  3. Designed a Figma website with a gallery and clear ordering flow.
  4. Created social content and ads from completed customer illustrations.
  5. Managed customer communication, orders, and delivery follow-up.

The solution

Hand-drawn portraits, before-and-after content, a gallery-led storefront, and personal communication from order to delivery.

Outcome

Illustrated 300+ pets by hand over two years and helped build PAW Studio into a trusted custom pet illustration brand. Visit pawstudio.co.id ↗

Three to four original pet photo and illustration pairs are needed for the gallery.

Overview

Supps247 is an Australian online supplement store offering quality products at affordable prices for gym-goers, athletes, and health-conscious customers.

The challenge

  • Communicate value without making affordable products feel cheap.
  • Stand out in a crowded Australian supplement market.
  • Make a large product range easy to navigate and shop.

The process

  1. Researched Australian supplement retailers and how they present pricing and product details.
  2. Defined a bold, energetic visual direction with consistent product presentation.
  3. Designed clear navigation, scannable product pages, visible pricing, and a smooth checkout path in Figma.
  4. Created ads and social content focused on deals and product benefits.
  5. Designed email campaigns for promotions, new products, and returning customers.

The solution

A clear storefront, deal-focused ads and social content, and branded email designs.

Outcome

Designed the brand's digital presence across website, ads, social, and email. The live site contains assets replaced by the client, so Figma screenshots will be the primary visuals. Visit supps247.com.au ↗
